=====
about
=====

``geometor.model`` is the foundational symbolic geometry library of the **GEOMETOR** initiative, created and maintained by **phi ARCHITECT**.

Built on ``SymPy``, it represents points, lines, circles, polygons, wedges, and golden sections with exact algebraic coordinates, powering the deductive constructions of ``geometor.elements``, the triangle center models of ``geometor.etc``, and the interactive canvas of ``geometor.explorer``.
