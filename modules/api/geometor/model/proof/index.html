geometor.model.proof¶

Deductive proof ledger, exact algebraic assertion verifier, and construction timeline.

Provides:

  • Step: Structured chronological step record for given, construct, intersect, assert, and conclude phases.

  • ProofAssertionError: Raised when an exact algebraic invariant check fails in SymPy.

  • ProofMixin: Equips Model with SymPy-verified Euclidean demonstration assertions (assert_equal(), assert_spread_equal(), assert_perpendicular(), assert_parallel(), assert_congruent(), assert_area_equal(), assert_collinear(), assert_concyclic(), assert_incident(), and conclude()).

Exceptions¶

ProofAssertionError

Raised when a deductive geometric assertion fails exact symbolic verification.

Verify exact equality of segment quadrances \(Q(s_0) = Q(s_1) = \dots\).

Parameters:

  • *items – Two or more sympy.geometry.Segment objects or (P, Q) point pairs.

  • by – Optional Euclidean citation (e.g., "Def.15", "C.N.1", "I.4").

  • label – Optional step caption.

  • focus – Optional sequence of elements to highlight during this step.

Returns:

The recorded Step.

Raises:

ProofAssertionError – If any pair of quadrances differs symbolically.
